# FleetTms Data Processing Agreement (DRAFT draft-1)

Between the fleet owner ("Controller") and Kastra Enterprises ("Processor").

1. **Roles.** The Controller decides why and how its staff, client and fleet data is processed. The Processor processes it only on the Controller's documented instructions to provide FleetTms. For account and subscription data, Kastra Enterprises is itself a controller.
2. **Subject matter.** Fleet, trip, location, expense, payroll, client and lease data entered or generated through FleetTms.
3. **Security.** The Processor applies the measures in its security policy: encryption, tenant isolation, access control, audit logging, backups and incident response.
4. **Sub-processors.** The Controller authorises the sub-processors on the published list. The Processor binds each to equivalent terms and notifies the Controller of changes. **[TO FILL]**
5. **Staff access.** Processor staff may access Controller data only with the Controller's permission (time-limited, read-only, logged) or where required by law.
6. **Assistance.** The Processor helps the Controller answer data-subject requests and complete impact assessments.
7. **Breaches.** The Processor tells the Controller without undue delay on becoming aware of a breach, so the Controller and the Processor can meet the 72-hour notification duty to the ODPC.
8. **Return and deletion.** The Controller can export its data at any time. After cancellation, data is read-only for 90 days and then deleted, except records the law requires to be kept.
9. **AI features.** Document reading and plain-English questions process only the Controller's own data; sub-processors used for AI may not train on it.
10. **Controller duties.** The Controller must have a lawful basis for monitoring its staff and must give drivers the monitoring notice before tracking begins.
11. **Law and disputes.** The laws of Kenya. **[ADVOCATE]**
