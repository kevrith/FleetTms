# Driver monitoring notice (DRAFT draft-1)

Shown to drivers and turnboys at first sign-in. They must accept it before using the app. Owners can reuse the wording in employment contracts.

**What is recorded.** Your location while a trip is active, odometer photos, fuel and expense entries, delivery photos and signatures, and basic information about your phone that helps confirm records are genuine.

**Why.** To run trips, prevent fuel theft and false claims, keep you safe, and settle disputes with clients.

**When.** Location tracking runs only during an active trip and stops when you end it. It does not run outside work.

**Who sees it.** Your employer, your supervisors and managers. If your employer's vehicle is leased from another owner, that owner sees location and trip details only if your employer's lease agreement allows it.

**Your rights.** You can ask your employer to show you, correct or delete your data, and you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

**Scores and alerts.** Driver scores and alerts help coaching. A person must review them before any disciplinary decision.
